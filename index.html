About

A bit about me

I'm Chandra — a computer science student at Sai University who learns by building things I'm probably not qualified to build yet.

It works out more often than it should. Survival School started as a way to make exam practice less boring and ended up teaching me databases, auth and deployment the hard way. Signal-Lite began as a simple question — how do chat apps actually work? — and turned into an education in security I'm still working through.

What I care about most is software that respects the person using it: fast, honest, and it doesn't break when the wifi does. That's the bar I hold my own work to — and when it doesn't clear it, I fix it.
